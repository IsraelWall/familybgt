FamilyBGT

FamilyBGT is a free family budget app. Plan fixed and variable expenses, track spending during the month, share one budget with your partner and see a monthly report.

Your data stays in your own Google Drive, in a Google Sheet the app creates for you. There is no server and no analytics.
